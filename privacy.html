Privacy Policy

How the iBOUNCE MUSIX website and app may handle information.

Draft for review before publication. This policy describes the intended app and website experience. Confirm the actual Android app permissions, third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, and data practices before publishing. Effective date: 7 November 2026.

1. Who we are

iBOUNCE MUSIX is a music and video player brand. For privacy questions, contact ibouncemusix@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

When you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

3. Website hosting and technical information

Our promotional website is designed as a static site. Our hosting provider and any external services embedded in the site may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and access logs to deliver and secure the website. Their own privacy notices apply.

4. App media access

The Android app is designed to play media files you select or make available on your device. Access to audio, video, or other files depends on Android permissions and the app version. We will publish a verified description of the permissions and any data transmission before release.

5. Analytics, ads and third parties

The current promotional website does not intentionally include a custom analytics or advertising script. External fonts and hosting infrastructure may receive requests. The Android app's SDKs, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and online integrations must be audited and disclosed here before release. We do not promise that no information is collected by the app until that audit is complete.

6. How information is used

Support emails are used to answer questions, troubleshoot issues, and improve the service. Technical information handled by hosting providers may be used to deliver and protect the website.

7. Sharing and retention

We do not publish your support messages. Email and infrastructure providers may process information to provide their services. We retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed for support, operational, or legal purposes; a specific retention schedule should be established before launch.

8. Your choices and rights

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have shared with us and request correction or deletion where applicable. Device media permissions can be managed in Android settings. Your rights depend on applicable law.

9. Children and changes

The app and website are intended for a general audience. We will review age-related requirements before release. This policy may change as features and providers change; updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

Email ibouncemusix@gmail.com or use our contact page.
