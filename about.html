01 / The beginning

Built for people who live through music.

iBOUNCE MUSIX is an Android music and video player built around a simple belief: your personal music library deserves an extraordinary experience.

From offline listening and playlists to immersive video controls and a Ringtone Studio that lets you choose your favorite part of a song, every feature is designed to bring you closer to the sounds you love.

Our vision starts with a distinctly African creative spirit and looks toward a global community of listeners.
